Background

Who I Am

I'm a Berlin-based Cybersecurity Officer, AI Governance Specialist, and Lecturer building a career at the convergence of information security, intelligent systems governance, and academia.

As CISO at Mediaire GmbH, I oversee enterprise-wide security operations — ISO 27001-aligned ISMS, cloud security on AWS, and regulatory compliance under GDPR, NIS2, and HIPAA. I'm actively deepening my expertise in AI governance, working with ISO 42001 (AIMS) and ISO 22301 (BCMS) frameworks to help organisations govern AI systems responsibly and build resilient operations.

Before Berlin, I spent years in India as an Assistant Professor at Parul University, teaching cybersecurity to 3,000+ students. I currently also serve as a Visiting Lecturer at BSBI Berlin. I hold an MBA in Data Science & Big Data Management from IU Internationale Hochschule and am preparing for doctoral studies in Cybersecurity and AI Governance.

Open to AI governance consulting, cybersecurity advisory, academic lecturing, and speaking engagements — in Berlin and remotely across Europe.
